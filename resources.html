You're Not Alone

If you're experiencing abuse, struggling emotionally or need someone to talk to, these independent UK organisations can offer specialist support.

Need immediate help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. If you cannot speak on a mobile, follow the operator's instructions and press 55 when prompted. Pressing 55 does not automatically send your location.

Your privacy matters

If someone monitors your device, visiting a support website may leave traces in browser history. A quick exit does not erase history. Consider using a safer device and follow specialist digital safety guidance.
